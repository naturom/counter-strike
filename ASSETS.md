# Asset sources

Dust II is a hand-authored reconstruction of the recognizable modern map layout, with approximate elevations and original procedural scenery. It is not an exact export of the Valve map. Research reference: [Valve’s Dust II presentation](https://www.counter-strike.net/dust2). No Valve geometry, textures, models, logos or audio are redistributed. The game is not affiliated with Valve.

## Surface textures

Poly Haven publishes these assets under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Included files are the 1K JPG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps, stored locally so gameplay does not rely on third-party texture requests.

## Original generated content

Building/weapon/avatar geometry, wood grain, signage and interface artwork are generated by this project’s code. Sound effects are synthesized with Web Audio; there are no sampled third-party recordings. Sky rendering uses Three.js’s Sky example under the Three.js MIT license.

## Hands and equipment poses

Original procedural gloved hands, fingers, sleeve geometry and woven fabric texture are authored in `held-equipment.ts`. Equipment grips and first-person framing use Counter-Strike screenshots as visual references, including the [Steam Community agent sleeves gallery](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3383026241) and [CS2 community screenshots](https://steamcommunity.com/app/730/screenshots/). No Valve hand or weapon models are included. Held models have separate first-person lighting and depth rendering; world pickups do not include hands.

## Equipment detail and sniper scope

`weapon-models.ts` authors distinct original rifle, pistol, SMG, AWP-style sniper, knife, frag grenade and C4 meshes. It includes receiver profiles, magazines, scope glass and adjustment turrets, brushed metal finishes, moulded polymer, grenade markings and electronics. The same equipment bodies are used for held weapons, remote players and world pickups. Visual scope reference: [Counter-Strike sniper scope screenshot](https://www.gamewatcher.com/mods/counter-strike-source-mod/amh-awp-map). The original 3×/8× scope uses a circular mask and a mathematically narrower world-camera field of view; it does not use Valve assets.

Knife attacks use original 440 ms forehand and backhand animation curves. The blade and gripping hand share a wrist pivot, the free hand retracts out of the blade path, and the ready pose is restored before the existing 500 ms attack interval. Reduced-motion preferences reduce the slash amplitude. Hit detection, damage and reach are unchanged.
